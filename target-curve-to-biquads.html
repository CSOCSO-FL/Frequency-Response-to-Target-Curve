Paste frequency and dB pairs from a Dirac target curve, or upload a .targetcurve / TXT file. This tool fits PEQ and shelving filters to the shape and exports REW/miniDSP-style biquad coefficients for the Target Curve PEQ generator.

1. Target curve

Paste pairs such as 10 2.5 or upload a complete Dirac target curve (including NAME, DEVICENAME, BREAKPOINTS and frequency limits). Uploading fills the box; click Generate biquads to fit it.

2. Fitting settings

Two filters start as low/high shelves; the rest are bell/PEQ filters. More filters can reproduce more complex curves but can also overfit. Output uses the REW/miniDSP plus-a1/plus-a2 convention.
Paste a curve and click Generate biquads.

Notes: Biquads are an approximation of the input curve, not an exact conversion. Check the plotted error, especially with sharp notches or very narrow peaks. The fitted response is measured across the logarithmic frequency range rather than just the original breakpoints. Export coefficients at the same sample rate that the receiving PEQ tool expects (normally 96 kHz). The output is compatible with a REW-style parser expecting biquad1, followed by b0,b1,b2,a1,a2. This is a local browser tool; no data is uploaded.
