Frequency Response to Target Curve

Convert a REW measurement into a Dirac target curve, with optional adjustments for treble roll-off and overall tonal balance.

REW export settings: Select Use custom smoothing → 1/6 smoothing, check Do not include any comments or headers in the export, and check Do not include phase in the export. Export units: SPL; delimiter: Space; number format: REW export format.

1. Frequency response

Upload a REW TXT file or paste frequency and SPL values. Your measurement must include the full 500–2,000 Hz range.

2. Conversion settings

The 500–2,000 Hz average is set to 0 dB. Leave all shaping adjustments at 0 dB to preserve the normalized response.

3. Optional Curve Shaping

These controls reshape the normalized frequency response by adding dB adjustments, especially for adjusting the high-frequency roll-off. Feel free to experiment, but I recommend leaving the low frequencies unchanged for now. I use this section to give my speakers more treble roll-off. As a starting point, try setting High transition to −0.5 dB and Treble / end to −3 dB. Also try Curved treble transition.
